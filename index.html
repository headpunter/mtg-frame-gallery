Frames they never had

Twelve Magic cards picked at random from Scryfall, ten creatures and two planeswalkers, each rendered on three card frames Wizards never printed them in.

Every image is built from the card's real art crop, rules text and set symbol, pushed through a Proxyshop Photoshop template by a script. Nothing is hand-edited. Click any card to see it large.

Renders come from one generic script driving the PhotoCraft CLI over Proxyshop's PSD templates, running in a container on a home server. Card data and art from Scryfall; frames and templates by the Proxyshop community. Magic: The Gathering is a trademark of Wizards of the Coast. This is a fan project, nothing here is for sale.
